# Pre-registered protocol — Finerenone for cardiorenal outcomes in CKD — Living Meta-Analysis

This protocol is content-addressed: its SHA-256 is recorded in `bundle.json`.
Change one character here and the recorded hash no longer matches — that is
the pre-registration proof.

## PICO
- **Population:** Adults with type 2 diabetes and chronic kidney disease
- **Intervention:** Finerenone
- **Comparator:** Placebo
- **Outcome:** Composite cardiovascular death or hospitalisation for heart failure
- **Subgroups:** By baseline eGFR and albuminuria category

## Primary estimand
Pooled effect measure across the included trials for the outcome above.

## Included trials (pre-specified)
- FIDELIO-DKD (NCT02540993, PMID 33264825, 2020)
- FIGARO-DKD (NCT02545049, PMID 34449181, 2021)
- FINEARTS-HF (NCT04435626, PMID 39225278, 2024)
