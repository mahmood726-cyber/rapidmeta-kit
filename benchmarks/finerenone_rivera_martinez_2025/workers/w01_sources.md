## Frozen original-benchmark PICO and selection protocol

- **Population (pooled review):** adults enrolled in one of three phase III placebo-controlled finerenone RCT families; the two diabetic CKD studies and the symptomatic HF study are **clinically distinct** and should not be described as three HFpEF trials. Do not generalize a pooled estimate indiscriminately to HFmrEF/HFpEF.
- **Intervention/comparator:** oral finerenone, trial-specific titrated dose **versus matched placebo** on background standard therapy. Do not assume identical dosing or concomitant treatments across families.
- **Outcomes defined by review:** primary CV death, HF hospitalization, all-cause mortality, renal failure; secondary any serious adverse event, acute kidney injury, investigator-reported hyperkalemia, regimen-related hyperkalemia, UTI, pneumonia. Separately verify reported HF incidence. Do not substitute the trial's CV/renal *composite* for an individual endpoint.
- **Design/search:** randomized trials of finerenone vs placebo with cardiovascular outcome reporting; publication English required for included RCTs despite no language limitation in database search; searched PubMed-MEDLINE and Embase-OVID through **2024-09-01**. PRISMA flow in main **Fig 1**; complete search strings reported in supplementary material **ESM 1/2, mapping not yet verified**. Original comparison includes **3 trial families only**, n=**19,027 analyzed** = 5,674 + 7,352 + 6,001. Review Methods: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10557-024-07666-x#Sec2 (sections Data Source and Searches, Study Selection, Outcomes); Table 1 original article.

## Provenance caveats and exact acceptance gates

1. **PICO/indirectness:** CKD+T2D trials explicitly exclude symptomatic chronic HFrEF, while FINEARTS requires symptomatic LVEF >=40% HF. The populations are **not interchangeable**. In particular, meta Table 1 characterizes FINEARTS by eGFR 30–60 dose bands, while original trial access synopsis reports **eGFR exclusion <25**; this may reflect a simplified dosing description and is **not evidence of an eligibility error**. FINEARTS dose initiation versus target must be distinguished.
2. **Time/version:** FINEARTS primary trial online release is **2024-09-01** (the review's last search day), and its NEJM record notes an **2024-09-13 update**. Freeze exact reviewed source version before interpreting post-cutoff information as available at time of review.
3. **Trial-family deduplication:** Count FIDELIO, FIGARO and FINEARTS once each. FIDELITY and FINE-HEART reanalyse overlapping participants; no independent fourth trial.
4. **Endpoint integrity:** A first-HF-hospitalization HR is not FINEARTS' total worsening HF event rate ratio. Neither the aggregate published review HR nor trial-level numbers may be converted to an alternate estimand without compatible source.
5. **NOT_ENOUGH_INFO:** Exact random-effects estimator; follow-up/population per outcome; trial-level per-endpoint HR, CI or RR 2×2 data; exact supplement contents; primary-paper figure/page values; dated registry histories; permissibility of full-volume programmatic text/data reuse; second extractor confirmation.
6. **Reproducibility:** No outcome-specific RapidMeta config, Kit build or independent numeric output was produced in this Worker 1 run. The separate pre-existing *generic example smoke-build* checkpoint is **not benchmark validation**. Do not treat published pooled numbers as independent results.
